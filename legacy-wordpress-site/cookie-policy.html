Cookie Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to make our website function correctly, for example remembering your preferences as you browse; to understand how visitors use our website, using analytics services such as Jetpack Stats and Google Analytics, so we can improve our content and design; and to support the security and performance features provided by our hosting and website platform.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly necessary cookies are required for the website to function and cannot be switched off, for example cookies that support basic security and site infrastructure. Analytics and performance cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website, such as which pages are visited and how long visitors stay, so we can make improvements. These are set by services such as Jetpack Stats and Google Analytics.

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third parties who provide services to us, including Automattic (our website hosting and Jetpack security/analytics provider) and Google (for analytics). These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

5. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies already stored on your device. Please note that if you disable cookies, some parts of our website may not function as intended. You can find out more about managing cookies at aboutcookies.org or allaboutcookies.org.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for legal or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated “last updated” date.
