Our Story

Built from the front desk, not a boardroom.

Our founder and CEO spent almost ten years working across the hospitality and hotel industry — and kept seeing the same gap play out at property after property. Important information about a stay was going unsaid. If it wasn't communicated at check-in, guests could go through their entire visit without ever knowing everything the property had to offer.

There were always too many questions and never enough time to answer them all. And not every member of staff was equipped, or confident, to sell the property the way it deserved — through no fault of their own.

With Informax, that changes. Everything a property offers — amenities, local knowledge, house rules, recommendations — brought together into one custom-designed information directory covering everything a guest should know about your offerings.

And unlike many guest-directory providers charging outrageous fees for the privilege, Informax is priced to make sense — so properties of any size can give their guests the full picture, not just the ones with the biggest budgets.
